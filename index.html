PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

I’m a Product Designer and UI/UX Designer with 10+ years of experience in product design and UI development, combined with 15+ years of experience in website design, development, hosting, and digital experiences.

I specialize in transforming complex business requirements into simple, intuitive, and scalable digital products across enterprise web platforms, mobile applications, dashboards, monitoring systems, transportation, telematics, logistics, and government solutions. My experience covers the complete product design lifecycle—from requirements gathering, user journeys, information architecture, wireframes, interaction design, high-fidelity UI, prototyping, design systems, and developer handoff to implementation and design QA.

With a strong technical foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, AngularJS, WordPress, and PHP, I bridge the gap between design and development, creating designs that are both user-focused and technically practical. I also have extensive experience creating responsive websites, corporate websites, client websites, HR websites, logos, icons, SVG assets, and visual design systems.

My web background extends beyond design into WordPress management, website management, website hosting, website maintenance, SEO, social media management, and digital optimization, giving me a broader understanding of how digital products perform beyond the design stage.

I enjoy solving complex UX problems, designing data-rich dashboards and operational interfaces, building reusable design systems, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and turning ideas and requirements into meaningful digital experiences. I also use AI-assisted design tools and modern creative workflows to accelerate ideation, exploration, and visual production.
