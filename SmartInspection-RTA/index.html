Business overview

The Smart Inspection Vehicle (SIV) is an AI-powered inspection solution for Dubai’s Roads and Transport Authority (RTA). It modernises and automates inspection across several departments, aiming to raise efficiency, cut manual effort and strengthen compliance monitoring.

While the vehicle patrols its area, AI-enabled cameras spot possible violations, asset defects, safety concerns and other non-compliance. The inspector is notified on a tablet app, then reviews, validates and submits the finding to the department that must act on it.

My design focus

Inspection cars in Dubai carry cameras and AI models. As the car drives its route, the system detects violations on its own: plates that match permit or alert lists, waste and overflowing bins, sand on the road, open manholes, faulty traffic lights, and more. The inspector’s job is to review, confirm and submit, not to hunt for problems.

A confirmed detection becomes a ticket owned by the right authority, with a live SLA. I designed the full experience: sign-in, daily plan, event review, tickets, assets, manual inspection, submissions and the performance dashboard.

The problem

Too many detections

Over a hundred a week arrive continuously. Without triage, the inspector drowns in them.

Many owners

Dubai Municipality, Traffic & Roads, Public Transport, Parking, Licensing and Right of Way each own different violations.

Unreliable network

Weak Wi-Fi and cellular on the road means a cloud-only tool would lose work.

How might we let an inspector review a high volume of AI detections quickly and safely, route each to the right department, and never lose a submission?

Users

Field inspector (primary)

Works from a tablet inside the vehicle, in short windows, in strong daylight, often one-handed. Needs big touch targets, almost no typing and clear status.

Department officers and supervisors

Receive tickets and watch SLA, review rate and the AI’s confirmation rate to judge quality.

What the system detects

I grouped use cases so the interface could reuse one pattern for very different data.

Vehicles (ANPR)

Plate and emirate recognised from the camera. Vehicle details and spot alert are matched against RTA permit records. An expired permit seen by ANPR becomes a violation.

Cleanliness (AI)

Scattered waste, overflow bin, bulky waste, sand on the road. Waste-inspector checks cover road cleanliness, footpaths and bus stops.

Road and assets

Road track events, manhole open or closed, traffic light functional, other vehicles. Asset checks are kept apart from anomalies.

Information architecture

Key screens

I kept the same data and RTA theme, then improved hierarchy, spacing, colour meaning and touch comfort.

White app bar

Transparent RTA logo sits on white, so it is never boxed or distorted.

Meaningful colour

Blue for action, red, amber and green only for severity and SLA.

Real components

Plates, thumbnails, pills and progress bars replace plain text.

Softer depth

Rounded cards with light shadows separate content without borders.

Sign-in

Photo-led split screen with the transparent RTA logo on a clean white panel. One clear action, and a calm banner when an unsent item is waiting.

  • Vehicle ID sits with login because each session belongs to a car.
  • Feature list reassures: live detections, few taps, works offline.

Today: plan before you drive

A stepper (Plan, Use cases, Pre-start, Health check, Start) with route cards that show a map, time, stops and distance. The backlog card is the loudest element, so unreviewed work is never missed.

  • Done and Overdue are colour-coded pills.
  • Ad hoc inspection is a floating action for the unplanned case.

Events: the review queue

Real plate component, thumbnail, severity and department on every row. Counters and a red “needing attention” action sit beside the list.

  • Unreviewed and Filters chips carry live counts.
  • By type bars show where the work is: ANPR, cleanliness, unclassified.

Tickets and SLA

Each ticket carries a coloured SLA bar and time text: red when overdue, amber when close, green when safe, so urgency is visible before reading.

  • Open 26, At risk 1, Breached 1, Closed 104 (30 days).
  • Breached has its own shortcut.

Manual inspection

Four numbered steps. Severity chips turn red, amber or green when chosen, so the choice is obvious. A waveform makes voice recording feel alive.

  • Reporter, time, ward and position are pre-filled.
  • Submit button activates once the type and severity are set.

Dashboard

Gradient KPI tiles, rounded bar chart with today highlighted, and stacked High/Medium/Low bars per department, all from the same data as the original.

  • Confirmation rate 73.7% doubles as an AI-quality signal.
  • ANPR violations use red to separate them from cleanliness.

All screens are designed for a landscape tablet mounted in the vehicle.

Design principles

Review, don’t re-enter

The AI captures. The human decides in one tap.

Offline first

Status is always visible. Nothing is lost.

Right owner, automatically

Department comes from the event type, not from the inspector.

Severity before volume

Colour and counts surface urgent work first.
